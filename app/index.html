    Roadmap to the platinum

    Do the stages in order. Story first (and the one missable spot in Chapter 1), then stranger missions, then collectibles and exploration, then activities and challenges, then the 100% cleanup. The platinum pops last.

      Missable trophies — don't miss these

      Only six trophies can be missed. One meditation spot is at Jason's house in Chapter 1; the five stranger chains open in windows tied to story progress. Do them when they appear.
